# Greater Philly Time Bank

A free, mobile-first time-banking marketplace for Greater Philadelphia. Neighbors exchange services using **hours as currency** — no money changes hands.

> Think Craigslist for time credits, with a Venmo-style ledger and built-in safety tools.

---

## What is time banking?

1 hour of your time = 1 time credit, regardless of the service.
You earn credits by helping others. You spend credits by receiving help.

---

## Coverage Area

- Philadelphia County
- Delaware County (Delco)
- Montgomery County (Montco)
- Bucks County

---

## Features (MVP)

- **Marketplace** — post offers and requests; browse/filter by county, category, availability, and hour range
- **Messaging** — safe in-app messaging with block and report tools
- **Agreements** — structured commitments with scope, hours, and mutual confirmation
- **Ledger** — append-only transaction history; transparent and auditable
- **Reviews** — double-blind, structured feedback designed to resist abuse and retaliation
- **Moderation** — fast enforcement tools with full audit logging
- **PWA** — installable on mobile; works on low-end devices and slow connections
